# Who Am I?

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the **Laboratoire IEMN, University of Lille** (CNRS UMR 8520). I received my Ph.D. from the **Laboratoire PhLAM, University of Lille** in 2023. 

# What do I do in research?

I am an RF and millimeter-wave engineer with five years of experience in millimeter-wave and sub-THz hardware and systems. My research interests include high-frequency integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) for future 6G wireless systems, signal generation, detection, modulation, flexible waveguide design, RF-photonic links, and VNA-based characterization.

My expertise includes:
*   **Hardware & Systems:** Designed and characterized flexible metal/dielectric waveguides and transitions (75-330 GHz) for 6G/aerospace applications. Optimized high-gain horn antennas and evaluated beam-steering mechanisms.
*   **Simulation & Fabrication:** Full-wave EM simulation (CST Studio Suite, HFSS, COMSOL), PCB design (KiCad, EAGLE), and 3D-printing for SWaP-C optimization.
*   **Characterization:** VNA-based S-parameter analysis, anechoic-chamber antenna testing, and high-resolution THz rotational spectroscopy.

During my Ph.D., I developed a millimeter-wave chirped-pulse spectrometer, integrating broadband RF signal chains, custom Arduino-based control systems, and advanced digital filtering to study molecules of atmospheric and interstellar interest.

# What do I do in other times?

I mentor Ph.D. students in waveguide design, RF characterization, and EM simulations. I also lead technical team discussions and assist with ongoing projects in the lab.
